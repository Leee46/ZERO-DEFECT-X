# ZERODEFECT X — Demo & Script Guide

## Primary Clickable Workflow Flow (Phase 1 Demo)

Follow this 5-step walkthrough to demonstrate the full defect intelligence capabilities:

1. **Command Center (Dashboard)**:
   - Observe top KPI cards: Total Inspected (1,248), Defective (37), Defect Rate (2.96%), Current Risk (HIGH).
   - Click **"New Inspection Workflow"** button.

2. **New Inspection Page**:
   - Select product **P1042-087** (Precision Gear Plate A4).
   - Select Machine Station **M03** (High-Velocity Stamping Press 03).
   - Click **"RUN INSPECTION"**.

3. **Inspection Details Page**:
   - Observe **DEFECTIVE** status, Defect Type: **Scratch**, Location: **UPPER-RIGHT SURFACE**, Confidence: **94.2%**.
   - Inspect bounding box overlay on component canvas.
   - Review live telemetry: **Vibration 4.8 mm/s** (Anomaly).
   - Click **"INVESTIGATE ROOT CAUSE"**.

4. **Root-Cause Intelligence**:
   - Review Probable Contributing Factor: **"Elevated Machine Vibration on Station M03"**.
   - Review Evidence Score (**84%**) and technical disclaimers.
   - Click **"View Machine Risk Monitor"**.

5. **Risk & Action Verification**:
   - Observe M03 Scratch Risk at **82% HIGH**.
   - Click **"Recommended Actions"** → proceed through Corrective Action & Reinspection screen.
   - Observe Defect Rate drop from **8.71% to 1.20%** (**IMPROVED**).
